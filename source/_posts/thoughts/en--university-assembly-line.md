Walking down the road, unnoticed by anyone, it suddenly struck me: the four years of university — one cohort leaving, the next arriving — feels a lot like an assembly line. A group of people full of expectations walk in, their passion ground down, or grinding away without even knowing why; then they choose to stay on the line — take the graduate school entrance exam, or be thrown into society to face life.

Year after year, the people leave and the buildings stand empty. In the end, no one cares.
